### A41-2055-01 (P0, app): the meter tap put the wall in the wrong place

**What happened.**
- 0:10: "This is my meter". The card changed to "Step a little closer to the wall / Then aim at your meter again".
  That is `aimAtWallForMeter`: the raycast found no vertical plane. The phone was already ~0.4 m from the meter.
- 0:13: the second tap was accepted. The hit came from `.estimatedPlane`, since the scene's `walls[0].source` is `tap`
  (`ScanEngine+Export.swift` `lineSource`). ISO was 3200 under a porch light.

**Where the anchor is** (`session.meter_anchor.pose_in_world` against the photos and planes in the same packet):
- In the close-up photos p00001–p00005, the anchor projects onto the meter's line of sight (`hi/anchor_sheet.jpg`, red
  circle) but **1.6 m from the camera**. The meter's glass (~0.18 m) spans ~880 px at fx 1475, which puts the real
  meter **~0.35 m** from the camera. So the anchor is ~1.2 m too deep.
- ARKit detected the house wall during the scan: a 6.1 m × 2.2 m `wall` plane, a 1.25 m × 1.65 m plane at the meter
  board, and two small ones. All four have normals within ~10° of each other, about (0.55, 0, 0.84).
- Signed distance to those planes:
  - Phone at the close-up: +0.36 to +0.42 m (in front, as it should be).
  - Meter anchor: **−1.09 to −1.15 m (behind the wall)**.
- The anchor's wall normal is (−0.25, 0, 0.97), **~46–48°** from the detected wall planes. The camera faced the meter
  square-on (its forward is anti-parallel to the detected planes' normals), so the tap plane's orientation is what's
  wrong, not the view.

**Why nothing caught it.**
- `markMeter` (`ScanEngine+Actions.swift:14-60`) accepts the first `.estimatedPlane` hit with no checks: not how far
  the hit is compared with where the phone is, not how the normal compares with the camera's facing direction, and not
  how it compares with detected vertical planes nearby.
- The wall frame is never re-fitted later, even though ARKit then detected a 6.1 m `wall` plane that disagrees by
  ~1.1 m and ~46°.
- On a non-LiDAR phone there is no depth, so `CoverageMap.sampledRows` can't notice that the "wall" it claims to see
  is really the house wall seen through an imaginary plane.

**What it broke downstream.**
- The ground task at the meter (A41-2055-03).
- The strip went almost all green right after the skips (1:35): 13 ft of "seen wall" on a line that cuts through the
  yard at 46°.
- Both ends (A41-2055-06).
- The result (A41-2055-02).

**Fix direction.**
- Prefer `.existingPlaneGeometry`. Take an estimated plane only after sanity checks:
  - the hit distance agrees with the close-up's apparent meter size (or is within ~1.5 m of the phone);
  - the normal is within ~25° of the camera's reversed forward direction;
  - the hit is within a few cm of any detected vertical plane that covers it.
  Otherwise say "Move the phone slowly side to side so it can find the wall" and wait.
- When a detected `wall` plane later covers the meter's pixel and disagrees by more than ~0.2 m or ~10°, re-fit the
  wall line to it, or at least flag the scan.
- Export the tap source's disagreement with the detected planes, so the server can widen the wall error (see -02).

- `walls[0].source: tap`.

**Fix direction.**

  with a reason). At minimum, wall backing and route should be `unsure`, not `pass`.
- App: don't offer "See it on your wall" or a 3D spot when the walk was refused in both directions. Show "We couldn't
  measure the wall. Try again in daylight" instead.
- App: send the plane-disagreement measure from -01 so the server can act on it.

### A41-2055-04 (P1, app): the "too dark" card
