# Homeowner consent (template)

Signed copies stay with the crew. They never go in this repository or in the project store.

The capture is a team measurement of the outside of the house so the crew can check where a battery could sit. It is not a customer install visit.

## What the crew will do

- Walk the outside with two phones (one without LiDAR, one with LiDAR) in daylight.
- Photograph the meter, the panel if it is outside or the garage door is already open, and the walls around them.
- Measure the walls with a laser and a tape after the photos, and measure the meter cover with a caliper. The meter seal stays untouched. The panel dead-front stays on.

## What is recorded

- Video and stills of the outside walls, the meter, and the ground in front of the meter wall.
- Phone motion used to place those frames. GPS is stripped before anything is stored for the project.

## What is left out

- No house number, street, mailbox, licence plate, or face in any frame. If one appears, that frame is dropped.
- The meter number is covered in photos and stored only as the word REDACTED.
- The project record names the house `real-001` or `real-002` and a US state. It does not name the street or the owner.
